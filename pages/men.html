About Men Ministry

Men Fellowship

Growing together through faith, fellowship and service.

The Men Ministry of Faith Assembly Church Muguga exists to encourage men to grow spiritually, build strong families and serve God faithfully.

Through fellowship, prayer, teaching, mentorship and community activities, we seek to raise men who live according to God's Word.

Our Focus

  • Spiritual growth
  • Family leadership
  • Fellowship
  • Mentorship
  • Community service
