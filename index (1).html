OUR CHURCH

A Place to Worship, Grow and Serve

Faith Assembly Church Muguga is a Christian community committed to worshipping God, growing in faith and serving people within our community and beyond.

We believe that church is more than a place of worship. It is a family where people can grow spiritually, build meaningful relationships and discover their purpose in serving God and others.

YOUTH MINISTRY

Young People. Strong Faith. Purpose.

The Youth Ministry of Faith Assembly Church Muguga provides a place where young people can worship, learn God's Word, build friendships and discover their purpose.

Through fellowship, worship, prayer and service, we encourage young people to become strong, responsible and Christ-centered members of the community.
